Who We Are

Galaxy Solutions is a professionally managed engineering, technology, and infrastructure company based in Bokaro Steel City, Jharkhand. We combine engineering expertise, field experience, technology development, project management, and technical services.

We specialize in executing civil construction, infrastructure, and engineering projects for Central Government, State Government, Defence Organizations, Police Departments, Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs), educational institutions, healthcare facilities, and industrial clients.

Our Engineering Approach
Research + Engineering + Technology + Quality + Execution + Continuous Improvement

We believe successful engineering requires more than construction or installation. Our approach is to understand the real requirement, develop the appropriate technical solution, execute it professionally, test performance, and provide long-term support wherever applicable.

Water Technology Systems

Engineering Better Water Solutions. We develop, supply, install, commission, maintain, and improve water-treatment and water-management systems for government, defence, healthcare, industrial, institutional, and commercial applications.

Reverse Osmosis (RO) Systems

Potable & Industrial

Water Treatment Plants (WTP)

Complete municipal and campus-scale water-treatment solutions including clarification, multi-grade filtration, purification, and disinfection.

Municipal & Campus Scale

Sewage Treatment Plants (STP)

Advanced biological & membrane STP solutions designed for treatment and reuse of domestic wastewater, strictly complying with pollution control norms.

Biological & MBR Tech

Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP)

Engineered industrial effluent wastewater treatment systems tailored around specific chemical and manufacturing discharge characteristics.

Heavy Industrial Grade
05. PROCESS WATER

Industrial Water Treatment

Customized process-water treatment systems engineered for thermal boilers, cooling towers, rinse stages, and heavy manufacturing plants.

Boiler & Cooling Towers
06. RECYCLE & REUSE

Water Recycling & Reuse (ZLD)

Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) and advanced recovery solutions designed to reclaim and reuse treated water economically and sustainably.

Sustainable ZLD Focus
07. SOFTENING SYSTEMS

Water Softening Systems

Ion-exchange softening solutions for boiler feedwater, residential quarters, commercial laundries, and institutions affected by extreme hardness.

Ion-Exchange Resins

Multi-Stage Filtration

Multi-grade sand, activated carbon, iron removal, and cartridge microfiltration units eliminating turbidity, organics, and heavy particulate load.

Activated Carbon & Sand

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to common questions about our capabilities and engagements.

What is Galaxy Solutions?

Galaxy Solutions is an engineering and technology-driven organization focused primarily on Water Technology, with additional capabilities in Defence Technology R&D, infrastructure, civil construction, and technical services.

What is your primary technology focus?

Our primary technology focus is Water Technology, including water purification, Reverse Osmosis (RO), Water Treatment Plants (WTP), Sewage Treatment Plants (STP), Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP), water recycling, and industrial water-treatment solutions.

Does Galaxy Solutions conduct Defence Technology R&D?

Yes. Galaxy Solutions is developing capabilities in Defence Technology Research & Development, including research, engineering, prototyping, testing, and technology development, subject to applicable legal, regulatory, and authorization requirements.

Do you undertake government projects?

Yes. Galaxy Solutions provides engineering, construction, infrastructure, water-treatment, renovation, and maintenance services for eligible government, PSU, and institutional requirements. We are registered with CPWD, BCD Jharkhand, WRD, JBVNL, and GeM.

Do you provide AMC services?

Yes. We provide comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) and technical maintenance services for applicable water-treatment and engineering systems, covering preventive maintenance, spare parts, and emergency response.

Where do you operate?

Galaxy Solutions is headquartered in Bokaro Steel City, Jharkhand, and has the operational capabilities to undertake suitable projects and technical assignments across PAN India.

Can organizations collaborate with Galaxy Solutions on R&D?

Yes. We welcome suitable research, engineering, technology-development, prototyping, testing, and collaboration opportunities from academia, research labs, and industry partners.
