## 🏛️ About This Website
This website was specifically designed and developed based on the complete 21-page official specifications of **Galaxy Solutions**, an engineering and technology-driven organization headquartered in **Bokaro Steel City, Jharkhand, India**.

### 🎨 Design Philosophy: Precision Engineering (Royal Blue & Eco Green)
- **Visual Theme:** Inspired by the GreenMatrix / Precision Water & Engineering Corporate Brochure:
  - **Primary Royal Blue (`#416ab0` / `#234b88`):** Headers, navigation, brand identity, and structural frames.
  - **Fresh Eco Green (`#408e3c` / `#2e712a`):** Empanelment seals, ISO-style verification badges, and key CTA highlights.
  - **Pure Water Sky (`#0284c7` / `#38bdf8` / `#e0f2fe`):** Wave accents, card tabs, and technical highlights.
  - **Clean Ice Alabaster & White (`#ffffff` & `#f4f8fd`):** Backgrounds with subtle engineering micro-grid patterns.
- **Signature Design Elements:**
  - Distinctive horizontal blue underline accents beneath all major section titles.
  - 4 circular feature nodes (Reliable Technology, Superior Quality, Complete Solutions, Trusted Partner).
  - High-impact Royal Blue capabilities showcase block with crisp white metric cards.
  - Bottom signature slogan bar: *"Engineering Water • Advancing Technology • Building the Future"*.
- **Content Integrity:** 100% Galaxy Solutions specifications preserved (Bokaro Steel City, CPWD, Defence R&D, Turnkey Water & Infrastructure). Zero third-party content imported.
